## Full description (Google Play)

```
Block Quest is a clean, modern block puzzle built for short sessions and long high-score hunts.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag one of three pieces onto the 8×8 board
• Fill a full row or column to clear it and score
• Clear multiple lines at once for bigger rewards
• Keep a combo going with consecutive clears
• Game over only when none of your remaining pieces can fit

FEATURES
• Smooth touch-friendly controls with placement preview
• Undo (limited per run) when you need a second chance
• Daily Challenge — a fresh seeded puzzle every day
• Best score saved on your device
• Works offline — no account required
• Mute toggle for sound effects

VISUAL STYLE
Premium, calm, and uncluttered: soft lavender tones, rounded blocks, and clear feedback when you clear lines.

Block Quest is designed as an original puzzle experience. Play at your own pace, chase a new best, and come back for tomorrow’s Daily Challenge.
```
