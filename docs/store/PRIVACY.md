# Block Quest — Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Effective date:** 2026-11-16  
**Product:** Block Quest (`com.blockquest.game`)  
**Status:** Draft for store readiness — publish a hosted URL only after legal review and your approval.

---

## Summary

Block Quest is an **offline-first** puzzle game. In the current build we do **not** require an account, and we do **not** include advertising or analytics SDKs (Phase 7 is not enabled).

---

## Data we store on your device

The app may store the following **locally** on your device (for example via WebView `localStorage`):

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Best score (Classic) | Show your personal high score |
| Daily challenge best (per day) | Track that day’s best |
| Mute preference | Remember sound on/off |
| Tutorial seen flag | Avoid repeating first-run tips |

This data stays on the device unless you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

---

## Data we do not collect (current build)

- Name, email, or account credentials  
- Precise location  
- Contacts, photos, or microphone  
- Advertising identifiers for ads (no ad SDK installed)  
- Analytics event streams (no analytics SDK installed)

---

## Internet permission

The Android package may declare `INTERNET` because the WebView/Capacitor shell commonly includes it. **Core gameplay does not require a network connection.** If future updates add online features, ads, or analytics, this policy will be updated **before** those features ship.

---

## Children

Block Quest does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The current build has no social or chat features.

---

## Your choices

- Clear app data / cache to reset local scores and preferences  
- Uninstall the app to remove local data  
- Use the in-game mute control to disable sound effects

---

## Changes

We may update this policy when the product changes. Material changes that involve new data collection (for example analytics or ads) will be reflected here before those features are enabled.
